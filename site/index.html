03 / THE PROTOCOL

A benchmark you can reproduce.

01

Time the whole job

The supervisor starts its monotonic clock before launching the worker. Imports, fresh Chrome startup, navigation, model calls, evidence collection, shutdown, and grading all count. Dependency installs do not.

02

Check the actual page

Success requires a loaded document, the expected URL, and a visible matching semantic heading. A separate read-only CDP collector records the final DOM and screenshot. An agent’s “done” is never the grader.

03

Keep the failures

Randomized, sequential agent order. Fresh Chrome profile for every attempt. No benchmark retries. Timeouts and errors remain in the denominator; missing credentials stay visibly unmeasured.

04

Compare the declared stacks

These are 50 navigation goals on ten live sites, not a general workflow or extraction benchmark. Agents use declared model stacks and pinned source commits. Jev’s transport is adapted to the shared Chrome launcher; its policy is unchanged.
